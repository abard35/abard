Forever Drawn To You

Romantic Power Ballad / Lifelong Love

The Story

Years go by. Hair turns silver. Time leaves its gentle lines on every face. And yet – when he looks at her, he still sees the woman who took his breath away the very first time.

“Forever Drawn To You” is a love letter after a lifetime together. It is about a beauty that time cannot touch, because it lives in the heart: the way she moved, the light she wore, the warmth of her hand. Every line time has traced has only deepened the love.

For everyone who is still in love after all these years – and for the one who still makes your heart stand still.

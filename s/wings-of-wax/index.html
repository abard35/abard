The Story

Remember Icarus? He was warned not to fly too close to the sun. He did it anyway.

“Wings of Wax” is the story of a love that promised everything – wings of gold and light, a kingdom in the clouds, forever. We aimed for the stars without looking down. And then the air got thin, the wings began to melt, and the ocean was waiting below.

A song about the glory of the climb and the silence after the fall – for everyone who once believed they could fly.

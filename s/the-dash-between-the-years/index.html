The Story

Stand in front of any gravestone and you’ll see two dates. The day someone arrived. The day they went home. And between them – a tiny line, barely an inch long. That little dash holds everything: the first steps, the laughter at the kitchen table, the sleepless nights, the hands that worked and the arms that held us.

“The Dash Between The Years” is a song for everyone we have lost – and a quiet question to all of us who are still here: What will our dash say? Not how much we gathered, but whose burden we carried, whose tears we dried, and how much love we left behind.

Live it loud. Love without holding back. Make your dash count.

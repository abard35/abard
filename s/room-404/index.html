Room 404

Dark Sultry Heavy Blues Rock

▶ Play on ABardYouTube

The Story

Rain on the hotel glass. Two strangers, no names, no morning to fake.

“Room 404” is a heavy blues rock song about one night that burns every reason away – a dark, sultry surrender to desire, where control is the first thing to go.

Musically, it hits from the very first second: a cold start on a driving riff, thick double-tracked overdriven guitars, roaring Hammond organ swells and a wailing blues lead guitar. On top, a raspy, powerful rock tenor – and in the shadows, a breathy female whisper that never lets go.

Turn it up. Lock the door.

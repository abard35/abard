The Story

What keeps you awake when the rest of the world is asleep? What would you burn your hands for?

“Passion” is a song about that fire – the flicker in the dark that becomes a flood, the hunger that never says “enough.” It builds cathedrals on shifting sands, turns a simple house into a golden throne and the brightest eyes to stone. It costs us everything – and gives us the reason to breathe.

For everyone who would rather hold the lightning than live in the calm.

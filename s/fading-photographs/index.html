The Story

Some memories are too heavy to carry, but too precious to let go. “Fading Photographs” captures the raw, unfiltered emotion of the 90s Seattle sound. A slow-burning rock ballad about the inevitable passage of time and the faces we lose along the way. Just organic drums, a crying guitar, and the truth.

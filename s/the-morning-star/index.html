Dark Sci-Fi Anthem / Cinematic Cyber-Symphonic Metal

The Story

Once he was the light. Now he owns the night.

“The Morning Star” tells the oldest story of rebellion – the radiant son who defied the crown and fell from heaven – reimagined as a cold, digital nightmare. No rage, no mercy: just a machine-like voice narrating the collapse of every kingdom, every temple, every truth.

Musically, this is Cinematic Cyber-Symphonic Metal at its darkest: an extreme industrial score with titanic percussion, sharp choppy guitars and a monotone robotic narrator – cold as steel, precise as code.

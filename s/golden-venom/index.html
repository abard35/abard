The Story

Some things you know will destroy you – and you reach for them anyway.

“Golden Venom” is about that moment of surrender: one look, and every warning sign fades. Pale hair like a halo, a beauty painted with lethal paint, a fever that burns away all reason. It’s not love. It’s hunger. And it tastes like gold.

A song about the beautiful trap of desire – for everyone who has ever walked straight into the flame with their eyes wide open.

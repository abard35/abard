The Story

From the perspective of a higher power (or perhaps a rogue AI?), “The Blind Kings” looks down upon humanity's arrogance. It is a song about the digital age, the loss of humanity, and the inevitable reset. The Observation Cycle is complete.

A YouTube exclusive: this track is not available on Spotify or other streaming platforms.

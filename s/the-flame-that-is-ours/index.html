The Flame That Is Ours

Dark Romantic Power Ballad

The Story

When the world was dust and frost, one person walked in – and everything that was broken began to heal.

“The Flame That Is Ours” is a dark, devoted love song about two souls ignited by one flame. A love the world called madness, a palace forged from steel and blood, a bond that no one else will ever fully understand. Fire and ice, sin and salvation, chaos and peace – all in one gaze.

A Power Ballad for the love that stands against everything. Let them doubt. Let them crawl. This flame is ours.

Lullaby For The Stars

Cinematic Lullaby / Celestial Ballad

▶ Play on ABardSpotifyYouTube

The Story

For billions of years, the stars have watched over us – burning through the cold and the silence, night after night, guiding every wanderer below. “Lullaby For The Stars” turns the roles around: now it is our turn to sing them to sleep.

A quiet, tender song for the ancient lights above us. Close your eyes and drift away beneath an endless sky.

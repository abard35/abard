80s Style Power Metal Ballad

▶ Play on ABardYouTube

The Story

“Love Will Haunt Me Forever” is a raw, emotional throwback to the glory days of Swiss Power Metal and 80s Rock. Big reverb, soaring guitars, and a broken heart.

This is a raw, unpolished recording (demo version). It has some grit, static, and “scars”—just like the story it tells. It wasn't meant to be released, but many listeners wanted to hear the full song despite the imperfections. So turn it up, ignore the static, and feel the emotion.

A YouTube exclusive: this track is not available on Spotify or other streaming platforms.

The Story

When was the last time you did something just because you wanted to – not because a screen, a trend or a stranger told you so?

“Dreaming (Your Own Life)” is a wake-up call. We scroll through a million perfect lives, trade our souls for hollow gold and let the world command our hands – until we forget the sound of our own heartbeat. This song tears the wires out, breaks the mirror and screams what we all need to hear sometimes.

Stop living someone else’s dream. Your own life is waiting – right here, right now.

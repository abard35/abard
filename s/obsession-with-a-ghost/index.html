The Story

Welcome to the darker side of memory. “Obsession With A Ghost” is a deep dive into the madness of grief, where holding on feels like an addiction. It’s heavy, atmospheric, and driven by a beat that refuses to let go.

This is a raw, unpolished demo recording. It captures the sound of a sanity wearing thin—rough edges, feedback, and unfiltered emotion included. It wasn't about perfection; it was about getting the ghost out of the machine.

So turn off the lights, put on your headphones, and let the devil walk right in.

A YouTube exclusive: this track is not available on Spotify or other streaming platforms.

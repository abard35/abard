What Love Should Be

Romantic Power Ballad

▶ Play on ABardYouTube

The Story

In a world that is spinning out of control, where do we find peace? “What Love Should Be” is a deeply emotional ballad about finding the truth beneath the surface. It is a song for the optimists. For those who believe in miracles (and in pink!). And for the love that turns ashes into gold.

When I Have To Leave

Power Ballad / Farewell Without Regret

▶ Play on ABardSpotifyYouTube

The Story

One day, every one of us has to go. The question is: what do we want to leave behind – tears, or a smile?

“When I Have To Leave” is a farewell without regret. It looks back on a life lived to the full: the wine and the rain, the storms we conquered, the friendships forged in steel and the laughter that made our lungs burst. And it makes one last wish to everyone who stays behind: don’t drown the world in tears. Raise a glass. Turn around – I’m standing right behind you.

For everyone we have lost, and for everyone we will one day leave behind. Just smile.

Aftermath

Epic Power Ballad

▶ Play on ABardSpotifyYouTube

The Story

Tracing lines in the dust of what’s left. “Aftermath” is a raw exploration of the wreckage left behind by “porcelain lies.” It’s a journey through the static hum of silence and the realization that some kingdoms are built on hollow thrones. It’s about the moment you stop fighting the ghost and start standing tall in the ruins.

Musically, this Cinematic Acoustic Power Ballad is built on a foundation of intimate, breathy verses and a soulful male tenor. It features a dynamic slow-build, starting with a melancholy grand piano and acoustic guitar, eventually escalating into a massive, anthemic orchestral chorus. No growls, just clear, powerful high notes and swelling strings that capture the epic scale of emotional release.
